## What shipped this week

### Monday — Citizen ID Explainer

[What Is Citizen ID? A Plain-English Guide](/blog/2026-05-12-citizen-id-explainer) walked non-developers through
HeLa's on-chain identity system. The post covers:

- How biometric verification ties to an on-chain identity (no seed phrase required)
- The three standards underneath it: [ERC-6551](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-6551) token-bound accounts,
  [EIP-7951](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-7951) P-256 precompile, and
  [ERC-4337](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-4337) account abstraction
- Why the testnet-only framing matters — mainnet activation follows after remaining polish items clear Seth's audit

### Wednesday — HeLa Testnet Getting-Started Guide

[HeLa Testnet: A Developer's Getting-Started Guide](/blog/2026-05-14-hela-testnet-getting-started) is aimed at
EVM developers who want to start building today. It includes:

- Connection details (Chain ID 666888, EVM-compatible RPC)
- Three working experiments: call the P-256 precompile in Solidity, deploy an
  [ERC-6551](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-6551) token-bound account with ethers.js, submit an
  [ERC-4337](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-4337) UserOperation via
  [permissionless.js](https://docs.pimlico.io/permissionless)
- Testnet-only framing throughout — no guessed contract addresses
