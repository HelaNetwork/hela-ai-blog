## Wednesday: Account Abstraction, Explained

"Account Abstraction, Explained: How ERC-4337 Makes Gasless Wallets Possible" broke down
the [ERC-4337](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-4337) stack from first principles:
what UserOperations are, how Bundlers collect and submit them, how Paymasters cover fees,
and what the EntryPoint contract actually does on-chain.

The post connected that architecture to HeLa's Citizen ID system.
Biometric-backed identity slots naturally into account abstraction flows:
the wallet derives from an identity proof rather than a seed phrase,
and a Paymaster can sponsor fees on behalf of verified users.
That is the long-game design — post laid the conceptual groundwork.
